# 🚚 Logistics & Sales Analytics Dashboard

## 📊 End-to-End Logistics Performance Overview

An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze sales, orders, customers, payments, freight costs, and delivery performance.

The dashboard provides an executive-level overview of logistics and sales operations, helping users identify revenue trends, customer and product performance, delivery efficiency, payment behavior, and review sentiment.

---

## 🎯 Project Overview

The objective of this project is to transform raw logistics and sales data into meaningful business insights through data cleaning, data modeling, DAX calculations, and interactive Power BI visualizations.

The dashboard enables users to monitor important business KPIs and analyze performance across different dimensions such as:

- Sales
- Orders
- Customers
- Products
- Freight
- Delivery performance
- Payment methods
- Customer reviews
- Geographic performance

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

The project was developed using the following technologies:

- 📊 **Power BI Desktop** – Dashboard development and data visualization
- 🔄 **Power Query** – Data cleaning and transformation
- 🧠 **DAX** – Measures, KPIs, calculations, and business logic
- 🗄️ **SQL** – Data extraction, validation, aggregation, and analysis
- 🔗 **Data Modeling** – Relationships between fact and dimension tables
- 📁 **PBIX** – Power BI project file

# 💼 Business Problem

Logistics and e-commerce businesses generate large volumes of operational and sales data.

However, raw transactional data does not provide management with an easy way to answer questions such as:

- How much revenue is being generated?
- How many products and orders are being processed?
- Which customer locations generate the most revenue?
- Which product categories perform best?
- What percentage of orders are delivered late?
- Which payment methods are most commonly used?
- What is the average delivery time?
- How satisfied are customers?
- How much freight cost is being generated?

The purpose of this dashboard is to provide a centralized analytical view of these metrics.

---

# 🎯 Dashboard Objectives

The main objectives of the dashboard are:

1. Monitor overall sales performance.

3. Analyze freight costs.

5. Identify late deliveries.
6. Analyze product/category performance.
7. Understand payment behavior.

# 📊 Dashboard Features

## 1. Executive KPI Overview

The top section provides a quick overview of the organization's performance.

Key metrics include:

- Total Sales
- Products Sold
- Total Freight
- Average Delivery Days
- Late Delivery %

Each KPI also provides comparison information against the previous month where applicable.

---

## 2. Interactive Filters

The dashboard includes interactive filters for:

- Year
- Month
- Product Category
- Order Status
- Customer State
- Late/On-Time Status
- Purchase Date

These filters allow users to dynamically explore the dataset.

---

## 3. Product Category Analysis

A bar chart displays the highest-performing product categories by revenue.

This helps identify which product categories contribute most to overall sales.

---

## 4. Order Status Analysis

The Order Status Share visualization provides a breakdown of orders based on their current status.

This helps monitor the overall order fulfillment process.

---

## 5. Geographic Revenue Analysis

The dashboard analyzes revenue across:

- Customer states
- Customer cities

The Top 10 Customer State by Revenue and Top 10 Customer City by Revenue visuals help identify high-value geographic markets.

---

## 6. Delivery Performance

The dashboard analyzes:

- On-time orders
- Late orders
- Average delivery days
- Late delivery percentage

This provides visibility into logistics efficiency and customer delivery experience.

---

## 7. Payment Analysis

Payment behavior is analyzed using:

- Payment type
- Payment value
- Average installments
- Payment trend over time

The dashboard compares payment methods such as:

- Credit Card
- Boleto
- Debit Card
- Voucher

---

## 8. Customer Review Analysis

Customer satisfaction is monitored using:

- Average Rating
- Positive Reviews %
- Negative Reviews %

This allows management to evaluate customer experience alongside operational performance.
